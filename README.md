# Awesome AI Concert Video

A practical collection of AI concert-video tools, portrait-to-video workflows and finishing resources. Choose a tool by the job: a fixed cast template, a custom scene, or an edit of footage you already have.

[简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) · [AI Tiny Desk project profile](profiles/ai-tiny-desk/README.md) · [Five-person workflow](guides/five-person-workflow.md)

## Choose your starting point

| What you want to make | Start here | Why it fits |
| --- | --- | --- |
| An Outstanding-style desk-concert clip with your own cast | [AI Tiny Desk](https://aitinydesk.net/) | A focused workflow with 1–5 portrait slots, fixed performer roles and a supplied performance reference. |
| A custom scene with more creative directions | [Higgsfield](https://higgsfield.ai/), [Runway](https://runway.com/), [Kling AI](https://kling.ai/) or [Luma](https://lumalabs.ai/) | Broader creative tools to explore when a fixed concert template does not fit your idea. These are workflow suggestions, not a benchmark or a claim that the tools produce equivalent results. Features, access and pricing can change.

## Concert and portrait-video tools

### AI Tiny Desk

[Open the studio](https://aitinydesk.net/) · [How it works](https://aitinydesk.net/how-to-make-ai-tiny-desk) · [Prompts](https://aitinydesk.net/prompts)

**A useful place to start for the five-person desk-concert format.** Assign your portraits to the lead singer, keyboard player, two backing performers and saxophone player. The studio supplies the selected visual reference and matching audio reference, keeping the setup focused on casting rather than writing a scene from scratch.

The browser prepares portrait crops locally before submission. The interface supports 5, 10 or 19 seconds and 720p or 1080p output, with task recovery and MP4 download. Rendering depends on provider availability and the site's daily capacity. Face consistency, singing and lip synchronization need review; this directory does not claim independently tested output quality.

For the song and source context, see [Outstanding Tiny Desk AI: song, concert and meme](https://aitinydesk.net/tiny-desk-meme). For a broader workflow comparison, see [AI Tiny Desk tools](https://aitinydesk.net/ai-tiny-desk-tools).

### Higgsfield

[Official website](https://higgsfield.ai/)

An AI creative suite for exploring image and video workflows. Consider it when you want to choose a different scene or creative approach. Confirm which reference inputs and controls are available in the particular workflow you select.

### Runway

[Official website](https://runway.com/)

A platform for generative visual work. Explore its current video tools when building an original performance scene rather than following a fixed desk-concert template.

### Kling AI

[Official website](https://kling.ai/)

An AI creative platform with video-generation workflows. Check the current model documentation for image inputs, duration, audio and regional access before planning a multi-person scene.

### Luma

[Official website](https://lumalabs.ai/)

A platform for creative AI workflows. Review the current video-generation and editing options for custom visual scenes; feature availability depends on the product and plan.

### Pika

[Official website](https://pika.art/)

A creative video tool worth exploring for short visual ideas and effects. Review the specific workflow before assuming it supports multiple portraits, a music reference or a particular output length.

## Editing and finishing

### CapCut

[Online video editor](https://www.capcut.com/tools/online-video-editor)

Use an editor to trim a generated take, add readable captions, balance an authorized soundtrack and prepare the final framing. Adding audio in an editor does not itself generate synchronized singing or correct a face that changes between cuts.
